FAQ

Is Aiyedrix detectable by interviewers?

Aiyedrix runs in a stealth window that doesn't show up in screen-share. It listens through your system audio so the interviewer never sees or hears it.

Which platforms does it work with?

Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, HackerRank, CoderPad, and any browser-based call. The desktop app also handles phone interviews via a second device.

Can I use Aiyedrix for coding interviews?

Yes — the Coding Copilot supports 30+ languages with hints, walkthroughs, and complexity analysis without giving away whole solutions until you ask.

Do you store my interview audio?

Audio is processed in real time and discarded by default. You can opt in to save transcripts to power your Interview Reports.
